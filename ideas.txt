Create Room Sequence:
Create Room with first Settings -> Select Mode (online)
Mode Selection under the Room View
Each Mode has its own setting
Modes:
Casual - Normal Song guessing with Points and Hookers
Year Guessing - Either Decade or Exact Year (kinda like Hitster)
Coop Mode - Try to go as far as possible as a Team, while the Songs get increasingly harder
Elemination Mode - Three Lives if you lose all of them you are out
Memory Mode - You get 5 Songs and you have to guess them after the time runs out
